Why DEGURBA?

Population density describes one pixel. DEGURBA also considers connected cells, cluster population, distance, permanent land, and smoothing. That makes it useful for settlement context, urban/rural stratification, sampling, service-area analysis, and contextual map styling.

It is deliberately separate from a future numeric populationcheck: never infer an exact count or density from these classes.

Read results carefully

class_share is the dominant source-class area share inside the T3 cell—not confidence. mixed marks a source boundary. A finer triangle represents that boundary; it does not improve on the source's 1 km scientific resolution.

The Level-1 source hierarchy puts water in its residual “rural grid cell” domain. Use surface to distinguish water, land, mixed coast, and unknown/nodata.
